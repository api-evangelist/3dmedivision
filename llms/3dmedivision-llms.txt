# 3D Medivision

> 3D Medivision (쓰리디메디비젼, Seoul, Republic of Korea) builds 3D/2D surgical video capture
> hardware — cameras, recorders and monitors that mount to surgical microscopes and endoscopes —
> and operates a family of medical and veterinary education products built on that footage:
> Veterflix / Global Veterflix (video-on-demand courses and webinars), VGTC (live 3D surgery
> streaming and hands-on courses), VET-TV (3D anatomy lecture system), Veter VR, and Surgflix.
> Business model is hardware sales, distribution agreements, subscriptions and training.

## Status of the machine-readable surface

3D Medivision publishes NO public API. As of 2026-09-05, API Evangelist found no OpenAPI,
Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, gRPC/Protobuf or WSDL contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card,
no developer portal, no SDK in any public package registry (npm, PyPI, RubyGems, crates.io),
and no GitHub organization. Every `/.well-known/` discovery path returned 404 on the hosts the
company controls. The Veterflix learning platform is a tenant on the third-party Airklass LMS
(file.airklass.biz) rather than software the company exposes to integrators.
